About us

Welcome to CodeBite, the food delivery platform designed to connect you with the best local restaurants. Our goal is to make ordering your favorite meals as quick and easy as possible, without unnecessary complications.

Our Story

CodeBite was born in Milan out of a simple frustration: dealing with clunky delivery apps and food arriving late. The idea took shape when Filippo, a software developer, and Elena, a local restaurant manager, realized they were facing two sides of the same problem. Customers were tired of unreliable services, and restaurants were struggling with overly complex management systems.

Together, they decided to build a platform that focused on what really matters: speed, transparency, and ease of use. Filippo developed the technical architecture to ensure seamless ordering, while Elena provided the insights needed to create a practical dashboard for owners to manage menus and incoming orders.

What started as a simple project to improve the local food scene is now a complete ecosystem. CodeBite connects people directly with their favorite meals, giving restaurants the straightforward tools they need to serve them efficiently.
